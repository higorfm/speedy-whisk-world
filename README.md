# Speedy Whisk World

A cooking game around the world, created by Sofia.

Pick your chef, choose a country, grab the ingredients and tools in 30 seconds, then cook against the clock. Customers rate your dish from 1 to 5 stars, and every dish you master wins new clothes and unlocks extra recipes.

- 7 countries: Italy, Thailand, Brazil, United States, Japan, Mexico and France
- 21 starting dishes (easy, medium, hard) and 35 extra recipes to unlock

## Play on iPad

Open the game link in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. It opens full screen with its own icon and works offline after the first visit.
